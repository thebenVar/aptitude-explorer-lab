How it works

Take a test. Answer questions at your own pace. A soft timer keeps things moving, but it doesn't erase your answer if time runs out. You can always go back and revisit.

See your results. You get a friendly snapshot of how you answered. Results are automatically saved in your browser — your study guide will find them automatically.

Learn from it. The study guide explains how to think through each question type (step-by-step, the trap to avoid, the answer). You can upload your results for a personalized review tied to your actual answers.

Privacy: Nothing is collected or sent anywhere. Everything runs in your browser.
