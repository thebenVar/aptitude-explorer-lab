AI/ML Aptitude Check For fun, not official

A short, gently-timed, just-for-fun set of questions exploring the kinds of thinking AI/ML uses — numbers, reasoning, statistical intuition, step-by-step logic, and reading charts. It is not a syllabus exam, and not an official assessment of any kind.

The login is only to keep bots out — there's no account and no data is collected. Just use:
Student → username student  password learn2025
(Mentor view → username mentor  password mentor2025)

Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere. At the end you can download a results file to keep or share with your mentor.

Before you beginWelcome

You'll answer 30 multiple-choice questions across five areas. Each question has a gentle timer shown at the top — if it runs out the test simply moves on, it does not wipe your answer. You can go back to revisit earlier questions.

  • Answer honestly and at your natural pace — there's no penalty for changing your mind.
  • No calculators, no notes. Some questions show a small chart or a few lines of pseudo-code.
  • It takes about 20–25 minutes.
