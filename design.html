Before you beginWelcome

You'll answer 30 multiple-choice questions across five areas. Each question has a gentle timer — if it runs out the test simply moves on, it does not wipe your answer. You can go back to revisit.

  • Answer at your natural pace — no penalty for changing your mind.
  • No calculators, no notes. A few questions include a small chart.
  • Some questions are intentionally tricky — the obvious-looking answer is sometimes wrong. Pause before committing.
  • It takes about 20–25 minutes.
